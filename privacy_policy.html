Your choices.

This policy explains the data handled by the LibraAI development edition. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Account and library data

LibraAI stores the account details you provide and the reading, study, library, support, and marketplace records you create. Passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text. Signed-in users can review their preferences and export their account data from the privacy settings page.

Local development data

The supplied development edition uses a local SQLite database and browser storage for preferences such as color theme. Clearing browser data removes browser-only settings; it does not erase server-side account records. Development demo accounts and sample catalog content must never be reused as production credentials.

External services

Live payment, AI, email, SMS, cloud-storage, and content providers are not enabled by the development package. Before enabling them, a deployer must configure their own accounts, review each provider’s privacy terms, and update this policy to describe actual data sharing and retention.

Your controls

  • Sign in to manage privacy and communication preferences.
  • Export your account data from the authenticated privacy page.
  • Contact support for account or data questions.

Production deletion, retention, cookie consent, and regional privacy obligations must be configured and reviewed for the actual deployment.

This development policy is transparent about the packaged prototype; it is not legal advice or a production compliance certification.
