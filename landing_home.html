Browse common questions

What can I do with LibraAI?

Explore books, manage your personal library, track reading, save annotations, use study tools, or work with library and creator features.

How do I find books?

Open Explore the library to browse the catalog. Search and filter books by title, author, genre, or topic.

Can I create a customer or merchant account?

Yes. Choose Create account, then select Customer or Merchant on the registration form. Merchant accounts open the creator portal.

How do administrators sign in?

Administrators use Sign in with an account provisioned securely by an existing administrator. Public administrator self-signup is disabled to protect the platform.

Can I use LibraAI for live payments?

Live payments are not enabled by this development experience. A provider, verified configuration, and production testing are required before accepting real payments.

Does LibraAI provide full text for every book?

No. Full-text access requires the appropriate rights. The development experience uses catalog metadata and authorized sample content.

Does the AI tutor use a live AI model?

Some tutor tools are demonstrations. A production AI provider and validation are required for live model responses.
