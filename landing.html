GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We have answers.

What can I do with LibraAI?

Explore books, manage a personal library, track reading, save notes, use study tools and explore library administration and creator features.

Is the website ready to accept live payments?

Not yet. Payment and fulfillment workflows require an approved provider, credentials and successful production testing before live transactions can be enabled.

Can I read any copyrighted book here?

Full-text reading requires a valid license or other appropriate rights. The included experience uses samples and development content rather than distributing unauthorized books.

Does the AI tutor use a live AI model?

Some tools are demonstrations. A production AI provider, secure configuration and evaluation are needed to enable and validate live AI responses.
